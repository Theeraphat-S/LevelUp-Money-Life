# Domain Context

## Language & Glossary

**AssessableIncome (เงินได้พึงประเมิน)**:
Gross income subject to taxation under Section 40 of the Thai Revenue Code (e.g. 40(1) Salary/Bonus, 40(2) Freelance/Service, 40(8) Business).
_Avoid_: Total Revenue, Gross Profit

**StandardDeduction (การหักค่าใช้จ่ายตามกฎหมาย)**:
Standard statutory expense deductions allowed against gross income before allowances (e.g. 50% max 100,000 THB for 40(1) and 40(2)).
_Avoid_: Personal allowance, Tax credit

**TaxAllowances (ค่าลดหย่อนภาษี)**:
Deductions permitted by law based on family status, insurance, retirement funds, and eligible expenses (Section 47).
_Avoid_: Expense deduction, Tax rebate

**NetTaxableIncome (เงินได้สุทธิ)**:
Assessable income remaining after deducting standard statutory expenses and tax allowances (`Assessable Income - Expenses - Allowances`), used to calculate progressive tax.
_Avoid_: Net salary, Take-home pay, Taxable base

**ProgressiveTaxBracket (อัตราภาษีเงินได้บุคคลธรรมดาแบบขั้นบันได)**:
Tiered tax rate brackets ranging from 0% (0–150,000 THB) up to 35% (>5,000,000 THB) applied to Net Taxable Income.
_Avoid_: Flat tax, Fixed rate

**WithholdingTax (ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย - ภ.ง.ด. 1/3/53)**:
Tax already withheld at source by employers or payors throughout the tax year.
_Avoid_: Estimated tax, Advance tax

**RetirementCap (เพดานกลุ่มการออมเพื่อการเกษียณ)**:
The combined maximum deduction ceiling of 500,000 THB across RMF, SSF, PVD/GPF, National Savings Fund (NSF), and Annuity Life Insurance.
_Avoid_: General deduction cap, Total limit
