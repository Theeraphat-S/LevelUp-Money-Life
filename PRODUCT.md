# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Working adults who want to manage monthly salary, daily expenses, savings plans, and recurring personal tasks in one desktop app. They use it during personal planning moments: after work, at month-end, or when logging daily spending.

## Product Purpose

LevelUp Money Life helps users calculate daily expenses, review historical spending, summarize monthly money flow, plan budget allocations, and complete daily checklist quests. Success means users can quickly see where money went, decide what to adjust next month, and keep financial habits consistent.

## Brand Personality

Game-like, motivating, clear. The app should feel like a finance command center with progress, quests, XP, and levels, while still keeping money data easy to read and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a bank website: stiff, corporate, cold, and visually boring. Avoid overloaded fantasy/game decoration that makes tables harder to read.

## Design Principles

1. Finance first, game layer second: money data stays readable and accurate; quests motivate behavior.
2. Month-end clarity: every screen should help answer “เงินหายไปกับอะไร?” quickly.
3. Habit loop: daily logging and checklist completion should feel rewarding with low friction.
4. Planning over guilt: summaries should guide next actions, not shame the user.
5. Desktop comfort: dense tables are acceptable, but controls must remain clear and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Use readable text sizes for dense financial tables. Do not rely on color alone for categories or quest states. Respect reduced-motion preferences for animations.
